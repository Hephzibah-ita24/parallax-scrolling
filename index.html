UNITED KINGDOM

The “City of London” is a tiny square mile with its own mayor and police force, separate from the larger Greater London around it.

Legend says that if the ravens ever leave the Tower of London, the kingdom will fall, so they’re kept there and well looked after.

The UK is made up of England, Scotland, Wales, and Northern Ireland, each with its own distinct identity, and in sports they often compete separately.

“Big Ben” is actually the nickname of the bell. The tower is called the Elizabeth Tower.
